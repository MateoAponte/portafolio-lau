# EcoGiro: ilustraciones de hallazgos

Generadas con la herramienta integrada `image_gen`, una llamada por ilustración.
Los PNG conservan transparencia. Los títulos y descripciones se renderizan en
HTML con las fuentes y tamaños existentes del portafolio, para conservar su
legibilidad, adaptación móvil y traducción. No contienen texto rasterizado.

## Prompt común

Create ONE PNG illustration for a UX portfolio research insight card about
EcoGiro, an inclusive plastic recycling app in Bogota. Style: friendly flat
editorial vector-like raster illustration, rounded clean shapes, medium
dark-green outlines, simple 2D forms, similar to small healthcare UX infographic
illustrations. Strict palette primary green #1E8F5B, pale mint #F0F9F6, cream
#FEFAEF, white #FFFFFF, beige #F7F3EA, helper gray #84827F, muted coral #E8B0A7,
light blue #D7E9FB. TRANSPARENT BACKGROUND. Square canvas, subject in bottom/right
area taking about 70% of canvas with generous transparent breathing room. No
text, letters, numbers, labels, typography, gradients, shadows, UI panels, or
full card background. The website overlays live Inter text separately for exact
font consistency. Clear polished illustration, consistent thick outlines, warm
human intent, not photorealistic, not 3D.

## Subjects appended to the common prompt

### Teach at the point of action

Subject: a smartphone scanning a clean empty plastic bottle, with a small leaf
and a simple three-tick preparation checklist. Make material-specific guidance
immediately after scanning visually clear. No text or numeric symbols.

Saved asset: `public/images/projects/ecogiro-insight-teach.png`.

### Make the handoff predictable

Subject: two human hands exchanging a transparent recycling bag containing clean
dry plastic bottles; a small simple inspection tag with a green check and a
separate muted-coral warning symbol conveys knowing material condition before
accepting. Friendly respectful recycler handoff. No glass, garbage mess, text,
or numeric symbols.

Saved asset: `public/images/projects/ecogiro-insight-handoff.png`.

### Reduce the learning burden

Subject: an older Colombian recycler in simple green work clothes holding a
smartphone with one large blank chat bubble and a green check, conveying a
lightweight messaging experience with minimal learning effort. Respectful
capable adult, calm confident expression, no stereotypes, no text or numeric
symbols.

Saved asset: `public/images/projects/ecogiro-insight-learning.png`.

These are explanatory illustrations, not interview photographs or evidence of
completed product tests.
